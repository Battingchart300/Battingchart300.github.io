About Me

I'm 16, from Slovakia. I study automechanik and automation, though honestly I think of myself as an electrician. It started with a file converter, I was just tired of uploading my files to random websites every time I needed to change a format, so I wrote my own. After that, a Docker setup that didn't take a whole afternoon to figure out. I'm also building my own assistant, JARVIS, the same way, piece by piece. My converter has 4 downloads so far. That's not a lot, but they're real people using something I made, and that still feels strange in a good way. Everything I build is open source, so anyone can read the code and run it. I don't really like tools that ask you to upload your files or make an account for something simple, so I build the opposite, stuff that runs on your own computer and doesn't send anything anywhere.
